# Reference: ADR Standards and Best Practices

An Architectural Decision Record (ADR) captures an important architectural decision made along with its context and consequences.

## Why Record ADRs?
- Prevents recurring debates over settled topics.
- Contextualizes decisions for new engineers and autonomous agents.
- Clarifies trade-offs accepted at the time.

## Rules for ADRs:
1. **Immutable once accepted:** If a decision changes later, create a new ADR that supersedes the prior one.
2. **Include realistic alternatives:** Clearly state why alternatives were rejected.
3. **Be honest about negative consequences:** Every architectural choice incurs trade-offs.
